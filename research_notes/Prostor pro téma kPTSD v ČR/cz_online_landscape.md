# Czech/Slovak online and social-media landscape on cPTSD and childhood trauma (as of Nov 2026)

**How this was researched and what limited it.** About 45 tool calls on 2026-11-06. Nearly all evidence comes from web-search results: the search tool returns page titles, URLs and a machine summary of the snippets. Direct page fetching was **blocked by this session's network policy** for every site tried that matters here: wave.rozhlas.cz, open.spotify.com, cs.wikipedia.org, podengine.ai, firstclass.cz and trends.google.com. For that reason:
- **Follower and listener counts were not checked for any Czech or Slovak account.** Each one is marked "neověřeno" (unverified).
- **Instagram, TikTok and Facebook-group contents are mostly not indexed** by web search, and closed FB groups, Discord servers and in-app search cannot be seen at all.
- Absence of evidence below means "not found via web search", not "does not exist".
- Some article dates are known only roughly, from snippet "days ago" values. These are marked "approx."

---

## 1. Who produces Czech/Slovak content on cPTSD and childhood trauma, and on which platforms?

### Takeaway
No Czech or Slovak social-media account built specifically around **KPTSD / cPTSD** turned up in any search. The Czech space for "childhood trauma" exists but is fragmented and sits next to cPTSD rather than on it:
- **Narcissism podcasts run by lived-experience hosts**, with linked Instagram accounts
- **Interview series with survivors** ("TRAUMA Z DĚTSTVÍ")
- **Therapist and clinic SEO blog articles** about KPTSD
- **Commercial "vnitřní dítě" (inner-child) courses**

cPTSD as a named, ICD-11-grounded topic shows up mostly in **blog articles of private practices and in university theses**, not as a social-media presence.

### Inferences
- Czech "childhood trauma" content clusters around **narcissism** (podcasts, Heroine articles) and the **inner child** (paid courses). Both frames are popular but sit outside ICD-11. No identifiable creator uses "KPTSD/cPTSD" as their brand or main topic.
- Written explainers of KPTSD do exist: roughly 10+ practice/clinic pages. A new account would therefore not be the first to *define* KPTSD in Czech. It could be the first to build a **sustained, social-native, survivor-facing presence** around it.

## 2. Credentials: professionals, coaches, or lived-experience/peer voices? Does anyone combine lived experience + academic psychology + peer work?

### Takeaway
The landscape splits cleanly in two:
- **Professionals writing static web articles**, plus guests in podcasts and media interviews
- **Lived-experience hosts without stated credentials**: the narcissism podcasts, the TRAUMA Z DĚTSTVÍ series, Klára Kučerová

Inner-child courses are mostly run by coaches or therapists with unclear credentials. **No Czech or Slovak voice was found combining lived experience of cPTSD + psychology studies + peer-worker role.**

### Cited Findings
- "Všechny odstíny narcismu" hosts present themselves through lived experience (daughters of narcissistic mothers) and invite psychologists as guests — [Podcast Republic](https://podcastrepublic.net/podcast/1805007571).
- Klára Kučerová is a lived-experience voice, not a clinician — [mojepsychologie.zeny.cz](https://mojepsychologie.zeny.cz/rozhovory/vyrostla-jsem-s-narcistni-matkou-z-hlubokych-traumat-mi-az-asistovana-terapie-ketaminem-3988).

### Inferences
- The "lived experience + psychology student + peer worker" triple is a **differentiated position**, as far as the indexed web shows. Peer work in CZ is new and contested, so this position comes with a credibility advantage and an ethical responsibility (boundaries, no diagnosing, supervision).

### Gaps
- Whether any Czech peer consultant publicly identifies with cPTSD on social media. Platform search is needed.
- The credentials of most Instagram-based "trauma coaches" (not indexed).

## 3. Dominant vs. missing angles

### Takeaway
**Dominant:**
- Narcissistic parent / narcissistic abuse
- Inner child
- Somatic / "the body remembers" (van der Kolk)
- Parentification
- Developmental trauma in children, aimed at teachers and foster care

**Under-represented or missing in the indexed Czech space:**
- ICD-11 6B41 criteria (DSO) explained to survivors
- Evidence-based treatment options and how to reach them in the Czech healthcare system
- Peer support for adults
- Adult survivors 30+
- Differential diagnosis from BPD/ADHD, as a survivor-facing topic
- CEN (emotional neglect)
- ACE

- One Czech source says the currently valid Czech classification does not distinguish KPTSD — [mindwell.cz](https://mindwell.cz/blog/ptsd-prubeh-lecba-stigmatizace).

### Inferences
- The clearest content gaps:
  1. Accurate, plain-language explanation of **ICD-11 6B41 and its real-world status in Czech clinical practice** (current sources contradict each other)
  2. A **practical guide to the Czech system**: who diagnoses, pojišťovna-covered vs. private therapy, waiting times, trauma-focused methods
  3. **Peer and survivor-to-survivor framing for adults**, as opposed to parent/teacher framing
  4. **Differentiation from BPD/ADHD** aimed at survivors
  5. **CEN/ACE** concepts, which have almost no Czech-language presence
- "Narcistní rodič" is a high-traffic entry point. A cPTSD creator could use it as a bridge while reframing toward trauma and evidence-based care.

### Gaps
- The real 2026 status of Czech MKN-11 implementation (whether 6B41 is codable in Czech healthcare) was not confirmed. Check uzis.cz/mkn-11 directly.
- No content analysis of Czech Instagram/TikTok was possible.

## Overall gap assessment, for the report writer (inference, based on all of the above)

The hypothesis "nobody in CZ has covered cPTSD properly" is **partly supported**:
- **Not supported:** that KPTSD is completely absent. There are about 10+ Czech web explainers, several theses, translated Herman and van der Kolk, Radio Wave pieces, and active narcissism and childhood-trauma podcasts.
- **Supported:** there is **no identified Czech (or Slovak) social-media creator focused on cPTSD**, no survivor-facing Czech guide to KPTSD diagnosis and treatment in the Czech system, no Czech peer-support community for adult survivors of developmental trauma, no Czech translation of Pete Walker, and contradictory public information about the diagnosis's Czech (MKN-11) status.

The adjacent space (narcissistic parents, inner child) **is** moderately occupied, mostly by lived-experience podcasters and coaches. The strongest open niche is: **evidence-based + ICD-11-accurate + Czech-healthcare-navigation + peer/lived-experience voice for adult survivors.**

Main caveat: Instagram and TikTok are poorly indexed. A manual in-app check of Czech hashtags is needed before concluding the social-media niche is empty.
