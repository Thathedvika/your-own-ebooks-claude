## 1. Status of ICD-11 (MKN-11) implementation in the Czech Republic

### Takeaway
As of late 2026, MKN-10 is still the binding classification for clinical coding and reporting in the Czech Republic. MKN-11 exists in an official Czech translation published by WHO/ÚZIS (first in 2024, updated in February 2026), but the switchover is not expected before about 2027–2028. The detailed Czech diagnostic requirements for Chapter 6 (mental disorders, the CDDR-type content) were still only "in primary translation" as of September 2025. In practice, Czech clinicians cannot yet officially code KPTSD (6B41). They have to use MKN-10 codes, which have no equivalent category.

### Cited Findings
- ÚZIS's MKN-11 page (as summarised by search): WHO published an **updated official Czech version of MKN-11 in February 2026**, prepared with ÚZIS and matching the 2026 release. **MKN-10 (10th revision) remains in force in the Czech Republic**, so the transition has not happened. — [ÚZIS – 11. revize MKN](https://www.uzis.cz/index.php?pg=registry-sber-dat--klasifikace--mezinarodni-klasifikace-nemoci-mkn-11) *(page could not be fetched directly; the claim comes from the search summary)*
- ÚZIS implementation plan: tasks scheduled for 2024–2026 include adapting health-insurer data interfaces, NZIS, DASTA and CZ-DRG, training ÚZIS lecturers (2024–2026), and legislative consultation (2025–2026). — [ÚZIS – Implementační plán zavedení MKN-11 v ČR (PDF)](https://www.uzis.cz/res/file/klasifikace/mkn/mkn-11-cz-implementacni-plan.pdf)
- A 2025 methodological presentation (Zvolský, published on akutne.cz, dated 2025-04) says the roadmap is planned to 2027 and **expected implementation is in 2028**. A precise switchover date needs the agreement of the institutions involved. — [Zvolský 2025 – Metodická příprava na příchod MKN-11 (PDF)](https://www.akutne.cz/res/publication/000604/attach-010-30-metodicka-priprav-ana-prichod-mkn-11-zvolsky-2025-04-27-12-17-40.pdf)
- An inter-ministerial working commission for MKN-11 was set up in September 2021 and normally meets twice a year. The plan and roadmap are updated every year. A 2023 presentation still referred to WHO's 2022 start with a 5-year transition period (i.e. to 2027). — [Daňková, ÚZIS 2023 (PDF)](https://www.uzis.cz/res/file/akce/20230615-ncmnk/04-dankova.pdf); [Daňková, Klasifikon 2023 (PDF)](https://www.uzis.cz/res/file/akce/20231011-klasifikon/klasifikon2023-blok-4-2-implementace-mkn11-v-ceske-republice.pdf)
- ÚZIS and WHO started translating MKN-11 into Czech in 2020, with clinical experts nominated by ČLS JEP. The translation covers the Foundation layer (~170,000 terms, of which more than 55,000 are MMS coding entries). — [ÚZIS – Co je MKN-11? Ať už je to cokoliv, máme to v češtině (Klasifikon 2023, PDF)](https://www.uzis.cz/res/file/akce/20231011-klasifikon/klasifikon2023-blok-1-1-co-je-mkn11.pdf); [Krejčová, Medsoft 2021 (PDF)](https://creativeconnections.cz/medsoft/2021/Medsoft_2021_Krejcova.pdf)
- **Chapter 6 diagnostic requirements (CDDR-type content):** a ÚZIS presentation from **September 2025** (Zvolský, "Definice onemocnění v MKN-11 kapitole duševního zdraví", Klasifikon 2025) says the published Czech version **does not yet contain this content** and that **primary translation is underway**. — [Zvolský, Klasifikon 2025-09-23 (PDF)](https://www.uzis.cz/res/file/akce/20250923-klasifikon/3-02-zvolsky.pdf)
- ÚZIS also produced an **unofficial ("neautorizované") mapping of DSM-5-TR to MKN-11** together with the Czech DSM-5-TR translators. — [ÚZIS – Mapování nosologických jednotek souvisejících s duševním zdravím (Klasifikon 2023, PDF)](https://www.uzis.cz/res/file/akce/20231011-klasifikon/klasifikon2023-blok-1-3-mapovani-dsm.pdf)
- Czech professional teaching material already lists "6B40 Posttraumatická stresová porucha / 6B41 Komplexní posttraumatická stresová porucha" in Czech (a 2021 conference abstract). — [mhconsulting.cz abstract (PDF, 2021)](https://mhconsulting.cz/abstrakt/__uploads__/mhconsulting-20211108094753638657.pdf)
- A clinic website warns that MKN-11 is "v Čechách dosud neplatné, ale očekávané" (not yet valid in Czechia, but expected) and that its own translation is not official. — [demosthenes.cz – Diagnózy](https://www.demosthenes.cz/diagn%C3%B3zy)
- **Example of confusion in academic writing:** a Charles University bachelor thesis abstract says CPTSD is part of MKN-11, which is "v České republice platná od února 2024" (valid in the Czech Republic since February 2024). This conflicts with ÚZIS, which says MKN-10 remains in force. The February 2024 date most likely refers to the publication of the Czech translation, not its legal validity. — [Kozíková, *Disociace a komplexní posttraumatická stresová porucha*, UK (dspace)](https://dspace.cuni.cz/handle/20.500.11956/194549); contradicted by [ÚZIS – MKN-11](https://www.uzis.cz/index.php?pg=registry-sber-dat--klasifikace--mezinarodni-klasifikace-nemoci-mkn-11)

### Inferences
- In 2026 Czech practice, a person meeting ICD-11 KPTSD criteria would be coded under MKN-10. The likely options are **F43.1 (PTSD)** or **F62.0 (trvalá změna osobnosti po katastrofické zkušenosti)**, or frequently a comorbid code such as F60.3 (emotionally unstable/borderline personality disorder), depression or anxiety. *This comes from the structure of ICD-10, which has no complex PTSD category. I did not find a Czech source that discusses KPTSD coding practice.*
- Because the Czech CDDR text for Chapter 6 was not yet published (status Sept 2025), Czech clinicians who want to use 6B41 criteria rely on the English WHO CDDR (2024), the 2025 ČPS guideline (see section 4) or secondary sources.
- The gap between "the Czech translation exists" and "the classification is valid" is itself a documented source of misunderstanding (see the thesis example above).

### Gaps
- I could not open uzis.cz directly to confirm the exact current (Nov 2026) official go-live date, or whether the Chapter 6 diagnostic requirements were published in Czech between Sept 2025 and Nov 2026.
- No Czech source found that explicitly says how KPTSD should be coded under MKN-10. The F43.1/F62.0 mapping above is an inference.
- No confirmation of the date of the first Czech MKN-11 publication (February 2024 is implied by the thesis abstract; February 2026 is the update according to the ÚZIS page).

## 2. Czech academic output: articles, authors, ITQ/ACE, prevalence data

### Takeaway
Czech peer-reviewed literature specifically on KPTSD is very thin. I found only two Czech-language journal reviews: Drozd 2014 in *Psychoterapie* and Schmidtová, Štěpánek & Čermák 2021 in *Československá psychologie*, which is about trauma and dissociation with KPTSD as one entity. The 2025 ČPS PTSD guideline also mentions it. The only Czech ITQ validation found is a 2021 MUNI diploma thesis (Sokol); I found no published validation article. Czech ACE prevalence data exist (Velemínský et al., 2017–2020, university-student samples), but there is no Czech population prevalence for KPTSD.

- Czech KPTSD scholarship comes mostly from theses and review articles; there are no Czech empirical clinical or epidemiological studies on 6B41. This leaves real room for a bachelor thesis, especially a well-done literature review, a knowledge/attitude survey, or an ITQ-based study building on Sokol 2021.
- The Velemínský ACE data (≈10% with ≥4 ACEs among students) give an evidence base for arguing that the population at risk of KPTSD is substantial in CZ.

### Gaps
- No NUDZ publication on KPTSD or on ITQ use was found. NUDZ trauma-research teams could not be identified from the available snippets.
- No Czech KPTSD prevalence estimate (clinical or general population) was found.
- I could not confirm the Sokol thesis results or whether a Czech ITQ translation is posted on traumameasuresglobal.com.

## 4. Czech training in trauma therapy and Czech Psychiatric Society guidelines

### Takeaway
The **2025 ČPS Doporučené postupy psychiatrické péče** has a PTSD-in-adults chapter that does mention complex PTSD. It recommends the ITQ for telling PTSD from KPTSD and a phased treatment model (stabilisation → processing → integration, with STAIR and skills modules). Trauma-therapy training in CZ is offered mainly by private institutes (EMDR, Somatic Experiencing, integrative trauma training, schema therapy, DBT). I found no state-accredited, KPTSD-specific curriculum and no central registry.

### Cited Findings
**ČPS guidelines**
- ČPS (ČLS JEP), *Doporučené postupy psychiatrické péče 2025 – Léčba posttraumatické stresové poruchy u dospělých*: first-line treatment is trauma-focused psychotherapy (prolonged exposure, CPT, EMDR; typically 8–16 sessions). Pharmacotherapy (sertraline, paroxetine, venlafaxine XR) is an alternative or add-on. Single-session debriefing is not recommended. CAPS-5 is the reference standard and PCL-5 is used for monitoring. — [postupy-pece.psychiatrie.cz (web)](https://postupy-pece.psychiatrie.cz/specialni-psychiatrie/f4-uzkostne-poruchy/lecba-posttraumaticke-stresove-poruchy-u-dospelych); [PDF 2025](https://postupy-pece.psychiatrie.cz/images/pdf/2025_DP_Lecba-posttraumaticke-stresove-poruchy-dospelych.pdf)
- The same 2025 guideline lists the **ITQ (ICD-11) for distinguishing PTSD from complex PTSD (DSO domain)** and describes a **phased model for complex PTSD with STAIR and skills modules**. The search summary noted that complex presentations are covered only briefly and that there is no separate 6B41 section. — [PDF 2025](https://postupy-pece.psychiatrie.cz/images/pdf/2025_DP_Lecba-posttraumaticke-stresove-poruchy-dospelych.pdf); [web variant](https://postupy-pece.psychiatrie.cz/specialni-psychiatrie/f4-uzkostne-poruchy/127-lecba-posttraumaticke-stresove-poruchy-u-dospelych)
- Earlier ČPS guidelines (Raboch, Uhlíková, Hellerová, Anders, 2014) are outdated. — [psychiatrie.cz PDF 2014](https://www.psychiatrie.cz/images/stories/deni_v_oboru/doporucene-postupy-4-2014.pdf)

**Training offers (provider self-descriptions; not independently verified)**
- **EMDR:** an older accredited EMDR training (accreditation from 2011, recognised by EMDR Europe) is no longer run, and the agenda was taken over by the association at emdr.cz. The Český institut pro psychotraumatologii a EMDR (ČIPE) appears as a training provider in the ČS KBT therapist directory. One therapist lists "EMDR Level 1 – Česká psychotraumatologická společnost, 2026". — [vycviky.cz EMDR](https://www.vycviky.cz/emdr-trauma-terapie/psychoterapeuticky-vycvik); [ČS KBT directory](https://cskbt.cz/adresar-terapeutu/); [terapie-dobris.cz](https://www.terapie-dobris.cz/)
- One therapist's CV lists a 75-hour psychotraumatology + EMDR training and a **30-hour course focused on complex trauma** (provider not visible in snippet). — [psychologie-adamove-jarka.cz](https://www.psychologie-adamove-jarka.cz/o-mne/)
- **Somatic Experiencing:** SomaBalance presents itself as the only SE trainer in CZ: a 3-year programme under SEI, ending with an EASE certificate. — [somabalance.cz](https://somabalance.cz/somatic-experiencing/); [somatic-experiencing.cz](https://somatic-experiencing.cz/)
- **Integrative trauma training:** SomaCare Institut (Šárka Weberová, Filip Žitník) runs a 3-year, 3×200-hour programme combining SE, EMDR, IFS and other approaches. — [somacareinstitut.cz](https://somacareinstitut.cz/sluzby/integrativni-vycvik-v-somaticke-terapii-a-praci-s-traumatem/); [integrace-traumatu.cz](https://integrace-traumatu.cz/)
- Biosynthesis training includes EMDR (ČIPE) and SE modules. — [biosynteza.cz](https://www.biosynteza.cz/o-biosynteticke-psychoterapii)
- **DBT:** Centrum Kaleidoskop says it was the first Czech team officially authorised to provide DBT, and it offers training and supervision. There is also British Isles DBT Training via Odyssea. — [centrumkaleidoskop.cz](https://centrumkaleidoskop.cz/); [ČS KBT directory](https://cskbt.cz/adresar-terapeutu/)
- **Schema therapy:** VIA SCHEMA Institut (ISST-certified trainers) and the Pražský institut schematerapie offer training. — [via-schema.cz](https://via-schema.cz/); [schematerapie.cz](https://schematerapie.cz/o-nas/o-nas/o-nas/certifikaty)
- Caution: one web source quotes a "2023 survey of the Česká asociace pro trauma" (28% of 142 registered therapists certified in body-oriented methods). **This could not be verified at a primary source and should not be used.** — [hledampsychologa.cz](https://hledampsychologa.cz/somaticke-priznaky-traumatu-telove-orientovane-terapie-v-praxi)

### Inferences
- KPTSD is formally acknowledged in the main Czech psychiatric guideline (2025), which partly contradicts the idea that "professionals ignore it". The coverage is brief, though, and the diagnosis cannot yet be coded (MKN-10).
- Training in methods relevant to KPTSD (EMDR, SE, DBT, schema therapy, STAIR) is privately run and fragmented. Whether a clinician knows KPTSD-specific approaches likely depends on which private training they took.

### Gaps
- No ISTDP-specific trauma training in CZ was identified (not searched in depth).
- STAIR training availability in CZ was not found.
- No ČPS guideline on BPD that discusses KPTSD differential diagnosis was found (not searched).
- The coverage of KPTSD/complex trauma in university psychology or psychiatry curricula (UK, MUNI, UP) was not researched (no syllabi accessible).

## 5. Peer work in Czech mental health care

### Takeaway
Peer workers (peer konzultanti / peer pracovníci) are an established part of the Czech psychiatric care reform. Training exists (CMHCD, the PodUP project 2025–2026, NGOs), but by 2025 the role still lacked firm legal and system anchoring (no standard job position in NSP/NSK, measure in NAPDZ 2020–2030). I found no trauma-specific peer support programme. Self-help groups for survivors of sexual violence exist, but a Czech resource reports no known self-help group for adult survivors of childhood sexual abuse.

### Cited Findings
- February 2025: psychiatric hospitals call peer workers "indispensable" but ask for better system anchoring. PN Bohnice supports creating an official "peer terapeut/konzultant" position in staffing tables. — [Zdravotnický deník, 2025-02](https://www.zdravotnickydenik.cz/2025/02/peer-pracovnici-jsou-pro-psychiatricke-nemocnice-nezbytni-volaji-ale-po-lepsim-ukotveni-v-systemu/)
- The Národní akční plán pro duševní zdraví 2020–2030 includes anchoring peer konzultanti in the system, including legislation and funding. Both job types were supposed to be entered in the Národní soustava povolání / Národní soustava kvalifikací by January 2023; whether this happened is unconfirmed. — [dobre-misto.cz – Jak zakotvit peer konzultanty do systému péče?](https://dobre-misto.cz/jak-zakotvit-peer-konzultanty-do-systemu-pece/)
- The **PodUP project** (Podpora zapojování uživatelů a pečujících) of the reform trains four types of peer workers, first part in H2 2025 and continuing in H1 2026. It is also creating a national platform for peer workers, which is to produce a methodology "Jak se stát peer pracovníkem". — [reformapsychiatrie.cz – Vzdělávání peer pracovníků v PodUP](https://www.reformapsychiatrie.cz/projekty/podup-podpora-zapojovani-uzivatelu-pecujicich/vzdelavani-peer-pracovniku-v-projektu-podup); [reformapsychiatrie.cz – Platforma pro peer pracovníky](https://www.reformapsychiatrie.cz/projekty/podup-podpora-zapojovani-uzivatelu-pecujicich/vznik-celorepublikove-platformy-pro-peer)
- Reform analysis of training and employment of peer consultants abroad and in CZ (2022/2023). It notes that understanding trauma matters for peer work. — [reformapsychiatrie.cz analysis (2022)](https://www.reformapsychiatrie.cz/sites/default/files/2022-10/Vzd%C4%9Bl%C3%A1v%C3%A1n%C3%AD%20a%20zam%C4%9Bstn%C3%A1v%C3%A1n%C3%AD%20peer%20konzultant%C5%AF_anal%C3%BDza.pdf); [2023 version](https://www.reformapsychiatrie.cz/sites/default/files/2023-02/KA2_Vzd%C4%9Bl%C3%A1v%C3%A1n%C3%AD%20a%20zam%C4%9Bstn%C3%A1v%C3%A1n%C3%AD%20peer%20konzultant%C5%AF_anal%C3%BDza.pdf)

FOKUS Vzdělávání course list ([fokus-vzdelavani.cz](https://fokus-vzdelavani.cz/nabidka-kurzu/)). ### Inferences
- The requester's combined role (peer worker + psychology student with KPTSD lived experience) sits in a gap: Czech peer work is framed around SMI/recovery, and I found no peer programme framed around complex or developmental trauma.
- A thesis or education project linking peer work and KPTSD (e.g. trauma-informed peer support) would likely be novel in CZ.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether a peer konzultant qualification or standard was entered in NSK/NSP by 2026, or whether the 2025–2026 PodUP training ran as planned.
- Current offers from Kolumbus (Liberec) and Fokus Praha peer training were not verified (sites not fetchable).
- No data on how many peer workers are employed in CZ in 2026.

## 6. Documented misunderstandings or debates in CZ (KPTSD vs BPD, over/under-diagnosis, media)

### Takeaway
Czech-language discussion of KPTSD vs BPD and possible misdiagnosis exists, but it is almost entirely in popular, blog or commercial therapy content and in theses. I found no Czech empirical study and no mainstream-media expert interview (iROZHLAS / Seznam Zprávy / Deník N) on KPTSD. The belief that KPTSD is widely misunderstood is plausible and indirectly supported: the diagnosis cannot yet be coded, Czech literature is scarce, a thesis misstates its legal validity, and popular sources claim it is often confused with BPD. It is **not supported by Czech data**, because no such data was found.

### Cited Findings
- Popular and therapy-practice sources claim CPTSD is often misdiagnosed as depression, BPD, GAD and others because of its broad symptom range. They say BPD shows rapid mood swings, while CPTSD shows more persistent low mood or numbness, and that careful history-taking is needed. — [Flowee – "Bitevní pole ve vlastní hlavě"](https://www.flowee.cz/civilizace/11123-komplexni-posttraumaticka-stresova-porucha-bitevni-pole-ve-vlastni-hlave); [zivotnahrane.cz (2025-06)](https://www.zivotnahrane.cz/2025/06/19/hranicni-porucha-osobnosti-zivot-na-emocni-horske-draze/); [blog-psychologie.cz – KPTSD a poruchy osobnosti](https://www.blog-psychologie.cz/post/komplexn%C3%AD-ptsd-a-poruchy-osobnosti-kde-kon%C4%8D%C3%AD-diagn%C3%B3za-a-za%C4%8D%C3%ADn%C3%A1-v%C3%BDvojov%C3%BD-p%C5%99%C3%ADb%C4%9Bh); [psychologie.cz – Na hranici vztahu a diagnózy](https://psychologie.cz/na-hranici-vztahu-a-diagnozy/)
- Czech therapy-provider and encyclopaedia pages on KPTSD: [dbterapie.cz encyclopaedia](https://dbterapie.cz/encyklopedie/komplexni-posttraumaticka-stresova-porucha/); [dbterapie.cz C-PTSD](https://dbterapie.cz/psychicke-obtize/komplexni-posttraumaticky-stres-c-ptsd/); [terrapia.cz](https://terrapia.cz/komplexni-ptsd-jak-lecit-dlouhodobe-trauma-a-zanedbani); [svetvklobouku.cz](https://svetvklobouku.cz/komplexni-ptsd-co-je-to-jak-se-lisi-od-bezne-ptsd-a-co-pomuze-v-terapii); [jsmekocky.cz](https://www.jsmekocky.cz/clanek/redakce/zdravi/kdyz-se-trauma-dostane-pod-kuzi-komplexni-ptsd-bere-vztahy-i-sebevedomi); [Seznam Médium (Jaroslav Král)](https://medium.seznam.cz/clanek/jaroslav-kral-komplexni-posttraumaticka-porucha-kdyz-traumatu-bylo-tolik-ze-se-projevuje-i-po-letech-25723); [podcast 13. komnata rodičovství, ep. 126 "Trauma a (C)PTSD"](https://13hrichurodicovstvi.cz/126-trauma-a-cptsd/); [natama.cz – Komplexní vývojové trauma (PDF)](https://www.natama.cz/userfiles/page/350/0bda2509cef85f057a1c0cff0b719916.pdf)
- Popular sources correctly note that DSM-5/DSM-5-TR do not include CPTSD as a separate diagnosis (unlike ICD-11). This WHO–APA divergence is cited as a reason the diagnosis is overlooked. — [svetvklobouku.cz](https://svetvklobouku.cz/komplexni-ptsd-co-je-to-jak-se-lisi-od-bezne-ptsd-a-co-pomuze-v-terapii)
- Academic treatment of the KPTSD–BPD overlap in CZ appears in Musilová's rigorózní thesis (UP 2019). — [theses.cz/id/i0l2pm](https://theses.cz/id/i0l2pm/?lang=cs)
- Documented factual error in academic writing: a thesis states MKN-11 has been "valid" in CZ since February 2024, while ÚZIS says MKN-10 is still in force. — [dspace.cuni.cz/handle/20.500.11956/194549](https://dspace.cuni.cz/handle/20.500.11956/194549) vs [ÚZIS](https://www.uzis.cz/index.php?pg=registry-sber-dat--klasifikace--mezinarodni-klasifikace-nemoci-mkn-11)
- Longová (MUNI 2022) states that comprehensive Czech publications on complex trauma are very rare, which is an expert-adjacent statement of a knowledge gap. — [is.muni.cz/th/z4o7i](https://is.muni.cz/th/z4o7i/?lang=cs&studium=258713)

### Inferences
- Structural reasons for misunderstanding among Czech professionals are documented: (1) the diagnosis cannot be coded under MKN-10 until about 2028; (2) the Czech Chapter 6 diagnostic text was not yet available as of 2025; (3) there are only two Czech-language journal reviews (2014, 2021); (4) KPTSD-relevant training is fragmented and private. Misunderstanding among laypeople is suggested by the large amount of commercial and blog content of uneven quality.
- The requester's belief is **plausible but not empirically documented for CZ**. Measuring it would itself be a strong, original thesis topic: for example, a vignette-based survey of Czech psychology students or clinicians on KPTSD vs BPD, similar to international vignette studies, or using the older UK PTSD-knowledge thesis as a template.

### Gaps
- No Czech empirical data on misdiagnosis or over/under-diagnosis of KPTSD or BPD.
- No mainstream Czech media interview with a named psychiatrist or psychologist specifically on KPTSD was found (searched iROZHLAS / Seznam Zprávy / Deník N terms without a hit).
- Czech BPD experts' views on KPTSD (e.g. from DBT centres or ČPS) were not found in citable form.
